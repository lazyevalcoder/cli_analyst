# These are a sample health indicators

Pipeline analytics

| Health Indicator | Why? |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Pipeline Coverage Ratio** | Compares total open pipeline value against revenue quotas (typically benchmarked at 3x to 4x). A low ratio signals a future revenue shortfall and top-of-funnel starvation. |
| **Stage Conversion Rates** | Measures the percentage of deals moving from one stage to the next. Identifies specific bottlenecks in the sales process (e.g., high drop-off between Proposal and Negotiation). |
| **Sales Velocity** | Calculates how fast deals move through the pipeline (Formula: `(Total Deals * Win Rate * Average Deal Size) / Length of Sales Cycle`). Highlights operational efficiency and revenue realization speed. |
| **Pipeline Stagnation Rate** | Tracks the proportion of open deals sitting in a single stage longer than the historical average without activity updates. Stagnant deals artificially inflate forecasts and waste capacity. |
| **Forecast Accuracy (Commit vs. Actual)** | Evaluates the variance between deals forecasted to close within a period ("Commit") versus actual Closed-Won revenue. High variance points to poor qualification rigor by sales reps. |
| **Win/Loss Ratio by Source** | Analyzes win rates segmented by lead origin. Ensures marketing and outbound teams are driving *qualified* pipeline rather than just volume. |

Revenue Intelligence Health Indicators

| Health Indicator | Why? |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Talk-to-Listen Ratio** | Measures rep monologue vs. active listening. Top-performing reps typically maintain a balanced ratio (around 43% talk / 57% listen), ensuring discovery is prioritized over pitching. |
| **Economic Buyer Engagement Rate** | Tracks the percentage of active deals where a decision-maker with signing authority is directly involved. Low engagement correlates with stalled deals and failed enterprise sales. |
| **Competitor Mention Frequency & Win Rate** | Analyzes how often and which competitors are brought up, cross-referenced with deal win/loss outcomes. Highlights market positioning vulnerabilities or product gaps. |
| **Next Steps Adherence Rate** | Evaluates whether documented next steps actually translate to scheduled meetings or tasks. Low adherence signals low buyer commitment and "happy ears" syndrome from reps. |
| **Buyer Sentiment Trend** | Monitors the trajectory of sentiment scores across sequential interactions for a deal. A downward trend serves as an early warning indicator for churn risk or lost momentum. |
| **Multi-Threading Depth** | Counts the unique number of stakeholders engaged per account. Higher multi-threading reduces single-point-of-failure risk if a champion leaves the target company. |

Sales Capacity & Ops Health Indicators

| Health Indicator | Why? |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Net Active Capacity vs. Target** | Compares total quota-carrying capacity (factoring in hiring pipelines, attrition, and ramp schedules) against the top-down corporate revenue goal. Identifies a capacity shortfall before the quarter begins. |
| **Ramp Efficiency / Time-to-Productivity** | Measures how quickly newly hired reps reach 100% of quota attainment relative to the standard ramp curve. Deviations highlight onboarding gaps or poor hiring profiles. |
| **Quota-to-OTE Ratio** | Evaluates the leverage of the sales model by dividing annual quota by On-Target Earnings (typically benchmarked around 4.5x to 6x depending on ACV). A low ratio implies an expensive sales force; too high implies unrealistic targets. |
| **Regretted Sales Attrition Rate** | Tracks the percentage of high-performing quota-carrying reps leaving the business voluntarily. High attrition destroys institutional knowledge, stalls pipeline, and spikes recruitment overhead. |
| **Rep Productivity Distribution (The 80/20 Rule)** | Analyzes the concentration of quota attainment across the team (what percentage of reps are hitting 100%+ of quota). A heavily skewed distribution signals systemic territory issues or an over-reliance on a few outliers. |
| **Cost of Sales Acquisition / Magic Number** | Measures go-to-market efficiency by comparing sequential quarterly revenue growth against sales and marketing spend. Validates whether adding capacity is efficiently translating into top-line growth. |

Compensation modelling

| Health Indicator | Why? |
| :--- | :--- |
| **OTE Realization Rate** | Measures the percentage of reps actually achieving their On-Target Earnings. If realization is consistently low (e.g., under 40%), it signals broken quotas or poor plan design that drives turnover. |
| **Cost of Sales / Compensation Expense Ratio** | Compares total sales compensation expense (base + commissions) against total Closed-Won revenue. Ensures that commission structures are sustainable and preserve gross margins. |
| **Accelerator Payout Concentration** | Tracks what percentage of total commission payouts are going to top performers exceeding accelerators. A healthy distribution rewards hyper-performance without creating runaway liabilities. |
| **Clawback & Dispute Rate** | Monitors the frequency and volume of commission adjustments, clawbacks, or payout disputes. High dispute rates point to ambiguous plan language, poor CRM data alignment, or premature crediting. |
| **Pay-for-Performance Correlation** | Evaluates the statistical correlation between individual quota attainment and total compensation earned. Weak correlation implies poorly structured plans that reward tenure or baseline factors over actual results. |
| **Earnings Disparity Ratio** | Compares the average earnings of the top decile of reps versus the median rep. Extreme disparity indicates a winner-take-all territory model or systemic inequities in lead distribution. |

Customer Expansion & Account Growth (Cross-Sell & Upsell)

| Health Indicator | Why? |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)** | Measures the percentage of recurring revenue retained and expanded from existing customers over a 12-month period (Formula: `(Beginning ARR + Expansion - Churn) / Beginning ARR`). The definitive metric for SaaS health and compounding growth. |
| **Expansion Attach Rate** | Tracks the percentage of active customer accounts that successfully purchase a secondary product (cross-sell) or upgrade tiers (upsell) within a given timeframe. Identifies how well the product portfolio naturally stacks together. |
| **Gross Churn Offset Ratio** | Evaluates how effectively expansion revenue covers revenue lost to customer churn and contraction. A ratio below 1.0 means the business is shrinking from within, requiring constant new-logo acquisition just to stand still. |
| **Whitespace Penetration Rate** | Compares current product adoption against the total addressable product catalog for an account. Highlights untapped cross-sell potential within existing logos that customer success or account management teams are missing. |
| **Time-to-First Expansion** | Measures the average duration between an account's initial go-live date and their first upsell or cross-sell event. Identifies whether customers need a longer stabilization period or if expansion motions are being initiated too late. |
| **Account Health Score vs. Expansion Propensity** | Cross-references product usage metrics, support ticket volume, and NPS with expansion conversion rates. Ensures that expansion pitches are timed when customer value realization is highest rather than forcing upgrades on struggling accounts. |

Pricing & Monetization Health Indicators

| Health Indicator | Why? |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Average Discount Rate & Variance** | Tracks the average discount given off list price and its variance across sales reps or segments. High or unchecked discounting erodes gross margins, cheapens brand value, and conditions customers to always negotiate. |
| **Price Realization Rate** | Measures the actual revenue captured versus total list-price potential ($Net\_Price / List\_Price$). A low realization rate points to weak negotiation power or a catalog price that is out of touch with market willingness to pay. |
| **Monetization Model Mix (Subscription vs. Usage)** | Analyzes the proportion of revenue driven by flat-fee subscriptions versus usage-based/consumption models. Helps ensure the pricing model scales symbiotically with customer value realization and business growth. |
| **Discount Approval Compliance Rate** | Monitors the percentage of deals that strictly adhere to pre-approved discounting matrices versus those requiring executive exception overrides. High exception rates indicate broken pricing guidelines or misaligned sales incentives. |
| **Annual Contract Value (ACV) Expansion via Packaging** | Evaluates how effectively new pricing tiers, add-ons, or packaging bundles drive higher average contract values over time. Measures the success of product monetization strategies. |
| **Price Elasticity / Churn Impact by Price Point** | Cross-references price increases or tier structures against subsequent customer churn rates. Identifies the optimal price ceiling where monetization is maximized without triggering a spike in customer defection. |

Churn Analytics Health Indicators

| Health Indicator | Why? |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Logo Churn Rate vs. Revenue Churn Rate** | Separates the percentage of accounts lost from the actual percentage of ARR lost. A higher revenue churn rate than logo churn indicates that enterprise or high-value accounts are defecting disproportionately, which is far more dangerous to the business. |
| **Net Churn Rate** | Measures the net change in recurring revenue over a period factoring in gross churn offset by expansion (`(Beginning ARR - Churn + Expansion) / Beginning ARR`). A negative net churn rate means the existing customer base is growing organically even without new sales. |
| **Early Lifecycle Churn Rate (First 90 Days)** | Tracks customer defection occurring within the first quarter post-onboarding. High early churn points directly to broken sales-to-onboarding handoffs, unfulfilled promises made by reps, or a critical failure in initial value realization. |
| **Involuntary Churn Ratio** | Measures the percentage of churn driven by systemic payment failures, expired credit cards, or billing errors rather than intentional customer cancellation. High rates can be mitigated easily with automated dunning management and updated gateway integrations. |
| **Usage Degradation Velocity** | Tracks the rate at which customer login frequency, active user counts, or core feature utilization drops prior to cancellation. Serves as the primary behavioral early-warning indicator to trigger automated Customer Success intervention. |
| **Top Root Cause Concentration** | Analyzes the frequency distribution of primary churn reasons. High concentration in a single category (e.g., missing a specific product feature or excessive pricing) gives product and executive teams immediate, actionable focus areas for retention. |
